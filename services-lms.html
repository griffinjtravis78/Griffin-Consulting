Digital Learning & Compliance Systems (LMS)

Griffin Consulting delivers specialized, state-compliant digital learning management infrastructure and operational software designed specifically for highly regulated industries, professional associations, and public sector organizations. Our custom LMS solutions bridge the gap between complex regulatory requirements and modern digital user experiences.

Engagement & Deployment Options

1. Partnership & Shared Growth Model

A collaborative engagement model designed for professional associations and organizations seeking to introduce digital education or continuing education (CEU) programming without initial financial friction. We provide the fully hosted LMS platform and technical infrastructure, working alongside your organization to build new digital delivery channels.

2. Managed Subscription & Platform Licensing

A dedicated software-as-a-service arrangement where organizations license our custom LMS and operational infrastructure for ongoing use. This option provides predictable administrative access, continuous platform hosting, security updates, and structured technical support tailored to your institutional needs.

3. Custom Development & Integration

A comprehensive, tailorable deployment for organizations requiring a fully branded, independent portal or specialized regulatory data integrations. We configure, customize, and deploy dedicated system architecture built explicitly around your workflow requirements and compliance standards.
